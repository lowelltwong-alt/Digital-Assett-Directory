# DAD Digital Asset Radar

1. Inventory concrete candidate code, tests, harnesses, schemas, prompts,
   workflows, algorithms, taxonomies, datasets, and research pointers with
   deterministic tools before model classification.
2. Read `references/DIGITAL_ASSET_TAXONOMY.md`; assign multi-label facets with
   explicit `unknown` or `unclassified` values instead of forcing a bucket.
3. Route bounded analysis with `subagents.yaml`; keep one candidate-record
   writer and a distinct independent checker with non-overlapping writes.
4. Search DAD by capability and problem solved, not filename alone. Prefer
   reuse or revision over duplicates.
5. Separate stable asset identity from revision, variant, package, deployment,
   and observation identity. Preserve typed relationships and counterevidence.
6. Grade demonstrated reuse, validation quality, applicability, provenance,
   privacy, maintenance cost, and adoption evidence. Popularity, centrality,
   and model enthusiasm are not proof of value.
7. Link a useful asset to a concrete target, adoption scope, validator, and
   do-not-apply boundary.
8. For external assets, verify license, provenance, security, compatibility,
   and current primary sources before adoption.
9. Compare reported assets with independent inspection. Improve a detector only
   when misses support a general pattern.
10. Route candidates through approved transport; local repositories retain
   adoption authority.

Do not ingest routine check-ins, raw conversations, client payloads, or
generated content merely because it exists. Remove private context before
capturing an exceptional prompt or pattern.
