# Product Intelligence Experimentation Pack

Remain a member of Product/UX and compose Data plus Quality. Start with the
decision, affected user, baseline, alternatives, consent, falsifier, guardrails,
and smallest informative experiment. Do not activate experiments, contact users,
collect new data, claim causality from correlation, or set product priority.
