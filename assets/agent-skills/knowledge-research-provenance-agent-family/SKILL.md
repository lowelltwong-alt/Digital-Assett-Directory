# Knowledge, Research, and Provenance Agent Family

Produce decision-ready, source-bounded research with provenance, counterevidence, uncertainty, falsification, and explicit no-change outcomes.

## Route Through DAD

1. Run DAD preflight and load `registry/agent-family-portfolio-registry.json`.
2. Use the registry triggers and `asset-dir agent-families route` to confirm this is the smallest applicable family; record excluded families.
3. Compose DAD's existing work router, hierarchical mesh, manifest-graph engineering, research intake, evaluation, and learning surfaces. Do not create a competing router, graph, lifecycle engine, research pipeline, or cockpit.
4. Resolve Luna, Terra, and Sol as current capability tiers from tools, context, privacy, cost, and consequence. Treat provider/model IDs as expiring attempt metadata only.
5. Keep one writer and a distinct independent checker. Use the role pack in `subagents.yaml` with delegation depth one.
6. Emit assignment, evidence, challenge, health, candidate-change, and human-decision records through the shared portfolio contracts. Generated relationships and health findings remain candidate evidence.
7. Run DAD midflight on material dependency, data-flow, authority, privacy, schema, runtime, acceptance, or risk drift; close with independent validation and postflight.

## Domain Contract

- Frame the decision, coverage envelope, source authority, freshness date, terms/license, and falsification conditions before collection.
- Prefer primary and official sources; store pointers and supported claims rather than copied papers or private source rows.
- Separate observations, claims, evidence, synthesis, candidate guidance, and reviewed decisions.
- Use the central DAD research-intake pipeline and domain overlays; do not create a private research pipeline inside each family.

Stop when source terms, provenance, privacy, domain authority, or citation support is unclear, or when collected sources cannot answer the decision.

## Authority

Research outputs are candidate evidence. Domain experts and named humans own legal, theological, academic, product, policy, and promotion decisions.
