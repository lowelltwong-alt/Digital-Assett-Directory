# Unfinished Intent Recovery Contract

This specialist pack prevents explicit material plans and partial builds from
disappearing across tasks, names, branches, worktrees, runtimes, or time. It is
part of Personal Project Management; it is not a new source of portfolio or
execution authority.

## Permanent boundary

An intent checkpoint or plan revision is recovery evidence only. Automatic
capture creates no idea, project, workstream, priority, dependency, reminder,
attention claim, lease, dispatch, task, branch, worktree, or execution right.
The constant output is `execution_authorized=false`.

Persist only structured, minimized plan results under an initialized private
`DAD_DATA_ROOT`. Never persist raw messages or conversations, hidden reasoning,
prompts or prompt hashes, credentials, client rows, source blobs, diffs, or
sensitive absolute paths.

## Capture and identity

For an explicit reusable family, library, product, architecture, or
multi-session build, write a compact seed after preflight and an immutable full
revision when planning completes. An interruption may leave only the seed.
Identical retry is idempotent; changed content creates a successor revision.

Search exact IDs and accepted bindings first, then reviewed aliases, content
fingerprints, repository and graph evidence, deterministic semantic cards, and
only then one bounded relation adjudication. Similarity is retrieval evidence,
never identity. Equal-best or authority-affecting ambiguity selects no winner
and creates one human decision candidate.

Keep semantic identity, revision, observation, source binding, relationship,
and decision IDs separate. Renaming revises one stable entity. Merges and splits
require an explicit human decision and preserve historical redirects.

## Recovery

Rebuild a recovery bundle from the exact current plan revision, stable
repository/worktree identities, HEAD/upstream/default branch, ahead/behind,
dirty/staged/untracked/conflicted signals, diff-stat and optional patch identity,
validation, acceptance, dependencies, consumers, observation time, and source
snapshot digest.

Unknown is never clean. Clean, contained, patch-equivalent, or closed task state
never proves completion. `verified_complete` requires acceptance evidence and
an independent check. A stale source digest blocks resume.

Recovery may propose inspect, resume request, preserve, handoff, reviewed
workstream attachment, hold, supersede, or archive candidate. It may never
checkout, commit, merge, stash, reset, clean, delete, rebase, push, change a
binding, dispatch, or close a plan.

## Weekly review

The Monday routine is a deterministic projection over the existing cockpit and
Today queue, never a second queue. It shows an inventory count and at most three
materially changed, due, or newly actionable decisions. Quiet, snoozed,
dismissed-unchanged, superseded, and lineage-copy items stay suppressed. Age
alone never wakes or ranks work.

Unchanged cycles use zero model calls. One low route may summarize changed
ambiguity only when separately enabled. Delivery intent, attempt, confirmation,
failure, and unknown outcome remain distinct; an unknown result is never blindly
retried. Schedule and toast delivery remain inactive until five supervised
read-only reviews over at least seven days satisfy noise, privacy, and
zero-mutation gates and Lowell explicitly activates them.
