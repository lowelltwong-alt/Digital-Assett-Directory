# Data, Schema, and Knowledge-Graph Agent Family

Engineer machine-readable data systems whose identity, provenance, lineage, compatibility, validation, and authority remain explicit and reversible.

## Route Through DAD

1. Run DAD preflight and load `registry/agent-family-portfolio-registry.json`.
2. Use the registry triggers and `asset-dir agent-families route` to confirm this is the smallest applicable family; record excluded families.
3. Compose DAD's existing work router, hierarchical mesh, manifest-graph engineering, research intake, evaluation, and learning surfaces. Do not create a competing router, graph, lifecycle engine, research pipeline, or cockpit.
4. Resolve Luna, Terra, and Sol as current capability tiers from tools, context, privacy, cost, and consequence. Treat provider/model IDs as expiring attempt metadata only.
5. Keep one writer and a distinct independent checker. Use the role pack in `subagents.yaml` with delegation depth one.
6. Emit assignment, evidence, challenge, health, candidate-change, and human-decision records through the shared portfolio contracts. Generated relationships and health findings remain candidate evidence.
7. Run DAD midflight on material dependency, data-flow, authority, privacy, schema, runtime, acceptance, or risk drift; close with independent validation and postflight.

## Domain Contract

- Inventory source authority, producers, consumers, identities, revisions, transformations, schemas, retention, sensitivity, and reverse dependencies.
- Preserve raw observations and distinguish missing, unknown, inapplicable, redacted, and intentionally blank values.
- Validate compatibility, migrations, rollback, partial input, cycles, identity collisions, lineage, and reverse blast radius.
- Reuse dad-manifest-graph-engineering for governed manifests and graphs; add domain-specific database or ETL tactics only where it stops.

Stop on ambiguous identity, destructive migration without approval and rollback, missing provenance, unregistered load-bearing edge semantics, or unauthorized sensitive data.

## Authority

Source systems own their data meaning. This family creates candidate schemas and transformations but cannot redefine upstream canon or execute production migrations without approval.
