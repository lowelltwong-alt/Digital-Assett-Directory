# Technical Product Company Operating System

The Technical Product Company OS is DAD's missing operating layer for a
one-person technical-product portfolio. It does not create a second project
manager, scheduler, cockpit, or source of truth. It composes the existing DAD
family portfolio, project-management operations, trigger radar, improvement
loop, daemon, and cockpit through a governed capability catalog.

## Scope

Machine Learning and Full Stack Engineering remain foundational families. The
first coverage portfolio is: technical-product architecture; quality,
verification, and release; security, privacy, and supply chain; platform,
cloud, and SRE; data, schema, and knowledge graph; integrations, APIs, MCP, and
automation; product, UX, and accessibility; observability, incidents, and
resilience; developer platform and DevEx; and product intelligence and
experimentation.

Platform is a top-level family because production runtime authority, validation,
freshness, and failure modes are distinct. Observability/Resilience,
Developer-Platform/DevEx, and Product-Intelligence/Experimentation begin as
specialist packs and can be promoted only by repeated cross-repository evidence.

## AI governance

AI rights are `observe`, `retrieve`, `recommend`, `draft`, `validate`,
`execute`, and `prohibited`. Consequence tiers are C0 through C4:

- C0: bounded observation or retrieval.
- C1: reversible local draft with no external effect.
- C2: governed material change requiring exact scope, independent validation,
  tests, rollback, and the named human gate.
- C3: external, production, spend, data-collection, or release effect requiring
  all C2 gates plus a verified, expiring execution lease, exact preview, and a
  separate final human confirmation.
- C4: prohibited regardless of model, confidence, frequency, or prior success.

Prohibited work includes unbounded autonomous self-modification, authority
bypass, secret movement, hidden persistent capture, irreversible destructive
actions, and final legal conclusions. A producer cannot approve its own
material output. A model upgrade never expands authority.

## Routing and graph

The generated catalog fingerprints every source descriptor. Routing applies
authority and eligibility before exact, lexical, optional semantic, validated
workflow, dependency, and validator expansion. It returns no more than three
primary capabilities, plus prerequisites and independent validators.

The typed graph separates families, specialist packs, capabilities, workflow
definitions, revisions, route decisions, executions, validations, governance,
freshness, conflicts, and supersession. Generated edges remain candidate
evidence; source registries remain authoritative.

## Evidence and anti-gaming

Telemetry is explicit opt-in and privacy-safe. Route and workflow-run ledgers
are append-only. Corrections point to earlier events. Raw prompts,
conversations, private source rows, client payloads, secrets, absolute paths,
and permanent provider-model IDs are rejected.

The projections keep eligible consideration, selection, execution, completion,
and independent validation separate. Synthetic trials never count in
operational aggregates. Unknown token or cost data remains unknown rather than
zero. Never-used capabilities are `unobserved`, not stale or bad. Frequency,
recency, centrality, similarity, provenance completeness, model confidence, and
self-grades cannot prove correctness or value.

## One-human operation

The capability snapshot feeds the existing portfolio cockpit. It exposes at
most three immediate decisions; the rest remain in a deduplicated digest with
evidence and drill-down. Quiet mode emits no immediate decisions. A no-change
catalog cycle requires zero model tokens. Routines remain dormant until the
human separately activates their exact scope, budget, cadence, notification
channel, and mutation class.

Cursor is represented only as an optional external executor adapter. It is
unavailable and unauthorized by default. An offload must be mechanical, bounded
to closed paths, based on stable authority and interfaces, deterministically
validated, isolated, reversible, leased, independently checked, and sanitized.
The adapter receives no additional authority and cannot install tools, change
global settings, access secrets, deploy, merge, or capture data persistently.

## CLI

Use `asset-dir product-os catalog`, `route`, `graph`, `routines`, `authority`,
`snapshot`, `record-route`, `record-run`, and `offload-check`. Route, graph,
routine, authority, snapshot, and offload checks are read-only. Recording is
explicit opt-in. `--publish` writes only the governed DAD snapshot,
operational graph, and routine baseline report; it does not schedule or
activate work.
