# Research and Standards Basis

This seed combines patterns from the user's repositories and internal working materials with established metadata and provenance standards.

## Standards used as design references

- W3C PROV-O — provenance entities, activities, agents, derivation, attribution, revision, and source lineage.
- W3C DCAT 3 — cataloged resources, identifiers, keywords, versions, status, rights, distributions, checksums, and qualified relations.
- W3C SKOS — controlled concepts, preferred/alternate labels, hierarchies, related concepts, notes, and mappings.
- FAIR Guiding Principles — machine-oriented findability, accessibility, interoperability, and reuse.
- RO-Crate — lightweight packaging of data, software, workflows, provenance, and authorship metadata.
- CodeMeta — shared software metadata for discovery, citation, version identity, and interoperability.
- SPDX — software, package, license, relationship, provenance, and build metadata.
- OpenTelemetry — a future-compatible model for traces, metrics, logs, and contextual observability.

## User-repository patterns carried forward

- deterministic AI front doors and read order;
- registry/schema/governance-first architecture;
- raw evidence must not directly rewrite stronger authority;
- append-only exception, audit, lesson, and handoff records;
- explicit source identity and revision lineage;
- master-register-driven work rather than repeated rediscovery;
- route, mode, allowed path, forbidden path, validation, and stop-condition preflights;
- human-reviewed candidate links and information-gain scoring from Orphan Radar;
- source/version registry, evaluation harness, regression library, failure taxonomy, context intake, escalation, and improvement-signal capture as high-compounding assets.
