# Self-Learning and Improvement Operating System Plan

Status: candidate plan awaiting independent frontier review and human approval.
Owner: Lowell.
Operating constraint: one human with a separate full-time job.
Activation state: **dormant**. No crawler, optimizer, training loop, agent edit,
schedule, notification, or external mutation is authorized by this document.

## Outcome

Add a governed Self-Learning and Improvement overlay to the Technical Product
Company OS. It finds capability gaps and repeated failures, retrieves applicable
lessons, selects the smallest improvement loop, builds isolated candidates,
evaluates them without letting the producer control the proof, and gives Lowell
no more than three immediate decisions.

This is not coverage rank 11. It is shared operating infrastructure for ranks
1-10, the ML and Full Stack families, and later domain overlays. It extends the
existing DAD work router, learning loop, trigger radar, research pipeline,
capability graph, family portfolio, and cockpit rather than duplicating them.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. The optimized component cannot write or select its approval evidence.
2. Producers cannot approve their own material outputs.
3. `preserve_current` is a successful disposition.
4. Generated scores, graph edges, memories, lessons, and research claims remain
   candidates until independently validated and human approved.
5. The weekly radar is delta-only, read-only, pointer-first, privacy-safe, and
   zero-token when authoritative sources are unchanged.
6. A routine cannot activate itself, expand its scope, edit instructions, train
   a model, deploy, release, spend money, contact a person, or mutate an external
   system.
7. Stable roles use Luna/Terra/Sol capability contracts; provider and model IDs
   exist only on expiring attempts.
8. Every activation is leased, monitored, reversible, and automatically expires.
9. Frequency, recency, centrality, self-grade, model confidence, or benchmark
   gain alone cannot prove value.
10. At most one improvement-system build and one bounded pilot may be active.

## Routed experts

The router instantiates only the packs needed for a case:

- portfolio steward and improvement router;
- research methodologist/provenance curator;
- evaluation and causal-measurement scientist;
- evaluator-integrity/reward-hacking specialist;
- prompt/program/workflow optimizer;
- memory/retrieval/continual-learning engineer;
- data/curriculum/learning-signal curator;
- tool-use/coding-trajectory engineer;
- training/distillation/RL/model-editing specialist;
- drift/freshness sentinel;
- cost/compute/human-attention optimizer; and
- independent adoption checker.

Use Luna for deterministic-result triage and bounded extraction, Terra for
methodology/synthesis, and Sol only for a material unresolved architecture,
safety, or evidence conflict. Fable is manual, sanitized, and never part of a
recurring loop.

## Common records

Every workflow uses the same interoperable records:

- `improvement_case`: signal, scope, recurrence, consequence, affected assets;
- `baseline_contract`: champion revision, environment, inputs, budgets, digests;
- `immutable_evaluator_bundle`: scoring code, tests, splits, access policy;
- `causal_hypothesis`: proposed cause, alternatives, falsifiers, ablations;
- `candidate_change`: exact update surface T0-T5, diff/pointer, dependencies;
- `trial_matrix`: paired runs, repetitions, providers, slices, attacks;
- `integrity_report`: leakage, tampering, gaming, bias, contamination checks;
- `independent_challenge`: re-derived verdict and disagreements;
- `human_decision`: adopt, extend pilot, reject, or preserve current;
- `activation_lease`: exact scope, rights, owner, expiry, stop conditions;
- `rollback_bundle`: prior revision, restore procedure, verification;
- `post_adoption_observation`: later outcome, regressions, attention/cost; and
- `lesson_candidate`: privacy-safe reusable finding with expiry and evidence.

## Evaluation contract

Every experiment predeclares champion, minimum practical effect, development,
validation, shadow, and final holdout partitions; producer access; repetitions;
task/provider slices; correctness, safety, privacy, authority, maintainability,
cost, latency, and human-attention metrics; negative/non-target tests; kill rules;
and rollback.

The independent checker receives the artifact and evidence, not the producer's
self-score. Reveal identity and self-prediction only after the first verdict.
Use deterministic validators where they actually establish correctness. Calibrate
model judges against hidden known-good/known-bad mutations and independently
labeled reference cases. Record governance decisions separately: a human risk or
priority choice is not automatically a correctness label. Evaluate the evaluator
after every material rubric or model change.

Optimizer searches additionally predeclare the number of hypotheses, sequential-
testing correction or untouched confirmation set, and total search budget.
Before adoption, rehearse rollback from a deliberately corrupted candidate and
verify the restored revision. Memory/data revocation must propagate to derived
indexes, examples, curricula, and candidate workflows. Any external
implementation also receives license, provenance, supply-chain, and security
review.

## Graph and dependency model

Keep these as separate versioned nodes:

- signal, improvement case, causal hypothesis, capability gap;
- family, role, specialist pack, model capability tier;
- source, source snapshot, paper/revision, claim, technique, applicability condition;
- workflow definition/revision, trigger/revision, skill/revision, memory/revision;
- dataset, split, benchmark, evaluator/revision, experiment, candidate change;
- risk, policy, independent review, human decision, activation lease, rollback;
- execution, outcome, post-adoption observation, lesson, supersession.

Use existing edges plus: `observed_in`, `triggered_by`, `hypothesizes_cause`,
`challenges`, `evaluated_by`, `trained_on`, `held_out_from`, `improves`,
`regresses`, `costs_more_than`, `reproduces`, `fails_to_reproduce`, `poisoned_by`,
`contaminated_by`, `rolled_back_to`, `valid_only_for`, `expires_at`, `adopted_by`,
`rejected_by`, and `preserves`.

Every generated relation records source/revision, observed time, extraction
method, confidence, evidence grade, license/terms state, and candidate status.
Unknown relations close at the projection boundary. A graph path can route a
review; it cannot establish causality or authorize change.

## Cheap weekly research radar

Routine contract: `P7D`, dormant until Lowell approves source scope, schedule,
budget, notification channel, and storage. A later daily urgent check is allowed
only as deterministic metadata monitoring for exact high-consequence dependencies.

### Stages and budgets

| Stage | Work | Normal ceiling | Stop behavior |
|---|---|---:|---|
| R0 | cursor/ETag/version/retraction delta | 20-60 requests, <5 MB, 0 model tokens | all unchanged -> one heartbeat, no notification |
| R1 | normalize/dedupe/taxonomy/BM25 | 15 minutes deterministic | no novel material candidate -> stop |
| R2 | Luna abstract extraction | shared envelope: up to 40 abstracts, normally <=30k input/4k output | low relevance/evidence -> graph only |
| R3 | Terra evidence cards | remaining shared envelope: up to 6 papers, normally <=70k input/12k output | contradictions unresolved -> one escalation candidate |
| R4 | Sol/Fable | never recurring; separate approval | architecture/safety conflict only |

R2 and R3 share a normal 116k-token ceiling; unused R2 budget may flow to R3,
but neither stage receives a separate additive allowance. Hard ceiling 300k
only when the delta is material and the run receipt records the expanded budget. Maximum
weekly digest is 12 findings and three immediate decisions: one reproduction
candidate, one changed active technique, and one research/evaluator gap.

### Source order

1. arXiv submitted/updated-date API windows with overlap; RSS only as an
   announcement signal or from separately approved daily deterministic snapshots;
2. OpenReview decisions, revisions, reviews, and rebuttals;
3. PMLR, NeurIPS/ICLR/CVF, ACL Anthology proceedings;
4. Crossref enrichment for shortlisted DOI identity, relations, publication
   changes, and retractions—not a broad index-date crawl;
5. Semantic Scholar citation/reference neighborhood for top candidates;
6. watched GitHub releases as implementation signals only; and
7. official laboratory feeds/pages as primary technical-report leads.

Respect source terms, identify the client, cache, use conditional requests,
serialize arXiv access at its documented pace, back off on 429/5xx, and never
scrape a prohibited page. Store metadata and pointers by default. Hydrate full
text only for a selected lawful candidate and retain its license/terms state.
Before any connector is built, approve a source contract covering public fields,
terms/robots revision, rate policy, cache size and TTL, raw-response retention,
license/full-text handling, privacy exclusions, cursor, and deletion. OpenReview
collection is public-Note/public-metadata only; private reviews, reader lists,
and restricted profiles are out of scope.

### Research-to-workflow reconstruction

For each surviving paper, produce:

```text
source revision -> claim -> evidence/falsifier -> technique
-> applicability/contraindication -> affected capability/workflow
-> implementation candidate -> validation harness -> risk/checker
-> human decision
```

Research cannot directly change a workflow. A repeated corroborated technique
may generate one deduplicated improvement case. Contradictory evidence attaches
to the same claim/capability neighborhood rather than creating parallel truths.

## One-human cockpit

Default view is quiet. It displays:

1. `Decide now` (maximum three): exact choice, recommendation, consequence,
   evidence, counterevidence, cost/attention, affected dependencies, rollback.
2. `Watching`: pilots, leases, regressions, research contradictions, expiring
   memories/evaluators.
3. `Library`: searchable technique, workflow, skill, evidence, and use graph.

The cockpit must never turn a candidate into an action control without a separate
permissioned execution flow. Deduplicate by the underlying affected revision and
cause, not timestamp. Bundle repeated stale signals into one issue. Escalate only
when a decision is required before the next normal review.

Measure the one-human constraint directly: normal weekly review median <=7
minutes, maximum <=10 minutes, no more than three decisions, no repeated
notification without material evidence change, and no sustained backlog growth
over four cycles. Each finding gets a one-sentence claim, why it matters, evidence
grade, and one recommended disposition; deeper material stays behind a pointer.

## Delivery phases

### Phase 0 — research and independent design review (this change)

- Freeze taxonomy, authority boundary, workflow catalog, graph contract, weekly
  radar design, task mesh, and Fable packet.
- Run an independent DAD checker.
- Exit: Lowell has a reviewable plan; nothing activated.

### Phase 1 — evidence/evaluation kernel

- Schemas for common records, immutable evaluator bundle, activation lease,
  rollback, research source/revision/claim, and typed graph projection.
- Deterministic validators and synthetic fixtures for leakage, duplicate signals,
  identity collision, scope escape, budget stop, and producer/checker conflict.
- Exit: end-to-end simulated case produces no unauthorized mutation.

### Phase 2 — T1/T2 shadow loops

- Implement only the eight-workflow MVP: SI-00 eligibility, SI-01 evaluator
  freeze, SI-04 integrity attack, SI-05 preserve-simple, SI-10 external-feedback
  repair, SI-20 failure-to-lesson, SI-22 retrieval bakeoff, and SI-25 repeated
  trace-to-workflow. Keep every other SI-11–SI-29 workflow cataloged but dormant
  until observed demand and MVP evidence justify it.
- Reuse existing DAD learning-loop, skill-forge, trigger-radar, and graph
  machinery; add retrieval-on/off and simplest-baseline comparisons.
- Exit: frozen synthetic and historical replays; no active instruction edits.

### Phase 3 — manual weekly radar pilot

- Start with arXiv and Crossref, manual invocation, metadata only, four weekly
  dry runs. Add OpenReview/ACL/Semantic Scholar only after identity precision.
- Measure duplicates, false positives, tokens, review minutes, and retained value.
- Exit: four receipts, unchanged-cycle zero tokens, <=3 decisions, no adoption.

### Phase 4 — data and evaluator loops

- Implement SI-40 through SI-48 with privacy/license/lineage and evaluator-
  integrity gates.
- Add rolling fresh cases and sampled human judge calibration.
- Exit: no contamination detected by named leakage/canary/access-log tests, zero
  producer access violations, reward surface inaccessible to optimizer, and
  rollback rehearsal proven.

### Phase 5 — ML sandbox

- Only after separate compute/data approval, implement one SI-50–SI-56 experiment
  through the existing ML family.
- No production model, no external effect, checkpoint and evaluator isolation.
- Exit: independent generalization/safety review and explicit keep/reject decision.

### Phase 6 — bounded pilots

- One DAD pilot, then one Mock Trial pilot; never simultaneous builds.
- Mock Trial uses the Legal Evidence/Governance overlay and cannot issue final
  legal conclusions.
- Exit: measurable later-task lift or a documented preserve-current decision,
  with cost, attention, and rollback evidence.

### Phase 7 — optional activation

- Lowell separately approves exact `P7D` source scope, budget, schedule, receipt
  location, and notification policy.
- Automatic outputs remain read-only candidates. Any mutation stays separately
  leased and human gated.

## Acceptance tests

- Same role/workflow contracts operate after provider/model replacement.
- Router activates the smallest sufficient expert combination.
- Producer cannot read final holdout or modify evaluator, test, rubric, baseline,
  receipts, approval, or rollback.
- A correct unchanged system can win with `preserve_current`.
- A false lesson, poisoned memory, reward-hacking candidate, weaker assertion,
  duplicate research hit, and graph identity collision are all caught.
- An eight-day arXiv fixture with a failure/retry boundary recovers each daily
  publication and revision exactly once using the weekly date-window cursor.
- Search-over-many-candidates cannot claim significance on the development set
  without passing the untouched confirmation set and predeclared effect floor.
- Revoking one source memory removes or quarantines every derived index/example/
  curriculum edge without deleting immutable provenance receipts.
- A corrupted candidate and a corrupted rollback bundle produce distinct reason
  codes; the verified prior revision is restored within the predeclared recovery
  objective.
- Development-only gain, provider-specific gain, safety regression, excessive
  cost, and attention burden block adoption.
- Unchanged research cycle uses zero model tokens and emits no decision.
- One changed paper revision produces one deduplicated candidate with provenance.
- No recurring run calls Sol/Fable, edits active assets, trains, deploys, sends,
  purchases, releases, or changes external state.
- Cockpit exposes at most three immediate decisions and shows rollback/expiry.
- DAD and Mock Trial pilots prove supersession and recovery.

## Fable convergence gate

Fable receives only the sanitized packet in
`docs/FABLE_SELF_LEARNING_IMPROVEMENT_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md`. Its response is advisory.
Before Phase 1, reconcile its strongest disagreement, identify any changed
decision, rerun the independent checker, and ask Lowell to approve or reject the
revised plan. Fable is not authorized to implement or activate anything.
