# Orphan, Activation, and Synergy Radar

## Orphan types

- **Hard orphan:** no graph connections.
- **Weak orphan:** too few or low-confidence connections.
- **Context orphan:** lacks description, owner, entrypoint, provenance, or intended outcome.
- **Need orphan:** declares missing capabilities that no cataloged asset currently satisfies.
- **Stale orphan:** old, disconnected, and unreviewed.
- **Intentional orphan:** intentionally isolated or archived; should be labeled rather than repeatedly flagged.

## Activation radar

For every dormant or low-degree asset, capture conditions such as:

- a missing dependency becomes available;
- a related new asset appears;
- a model or tool gains a required capability;
- a license or risk boundary changes;
- a repeated lesson establishes demand;
- new data, standards, or source material becomes available;
- a new user goal matches the asset's latent capability.

When a new scan or idea matches those conditions, the system creates an alert rather than silently promoting the asset.

## Synergy model

A pair or bundle receives a candidate score from:

- complementary capabilities;
- needs covered;
- shared outcomes;
- cross-domain novelty;
- verification and provenance;
- freshness;
- estimated integration cost and risk.

Each recommendation must state:

- why the combination may work;
- what it would produce;
- what is still missing;
- the smallest bounded experiment;
- evidence and confidence;
- reasons not to combine.

## Avoiding false confidence

Similarity, centrality, and information gain are routing signals. They do not establish truth, correctness, or strategic value. Review samples and calibrate against actual successful reuse.
